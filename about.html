    Our story

    Fashion built the way tailoring has always worked here.

    Where we started

    Adaeze Atelier began as a single rail in a shared shop on Zik Avenue, built on the belief that Awka deserved a wardrobe as considered as anything found in Lagos or further afield — without losing what makes tailoring here distinct: precision, patience, and a relationship with the person wearing the finished piece.

    How we work

    Every collection is developed with tailors and leatherworkers we know by name, in runs small enough that a pattern-cutter can still check the seams by hand. We would rather sell out of a piece than overproduce it.

    What we use

    Linen, brushed wool, vegetable-tanned leather, and silk-blend fabrics chosen first for how they wear over a year, not just how they photograph on day one. Hardware is solid brass or gold-plated, not painted.

    Who it's for

    People who want their clothes to do quiet work — pieces that hold their shape through a full day, travel well, and don't need to shout to be noticed. Dressing well, to us, is a form of self-respect, not performance.
